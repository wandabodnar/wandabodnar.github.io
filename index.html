PROFILE

About me

I am a marine scientist with experience across academia, offshore industry and the charity sector.

Since 2016, I have worked on aquatic habitat conservation, restoration and climate resilience in the Thames Estuary.

My current doctoral research builds on that applied experience.

Alongside my scientific work, I have a formal teaching qualification and share aquatic ecology through guest lectures, public talks, educational activities, community engagement and ecology-focused stand up paddleboarding.
